# CSA Corporate Arms & Identity Constitution

Version 1.0 — 6 October 2026

Cyber Security Agency Australia Pty Limited (CSA) adopts the two-eagle CSA Corporate Arms as its principal institutional identity. The arms are a private corporate emblem and must not be represented as the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, an Australian Government insignia, or evidence of government endorsement.

## Canonical elements
- Two eagle supporters.
- Central shield with W/WOS constitutional authority symbol.
- Eight-point proprietary navigation/star device.
- Motto: PROTECT | ENABLE | SECURE | ADVANCE.
- Navy, royal blue, steel, silver and white as primary colours; gold only as a restrained accent.

## Change control
Changes to the supporters, W/WOS symbol, star, motto, palette, entrance, seal or legal identity require Founder approval and a recorded version update.
