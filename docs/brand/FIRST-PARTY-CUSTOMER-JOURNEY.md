# First-party customer journey

Date: 12 October 2026
Authority: Founder instruction in the CSA project conversation.
Status: Directive recorded; immediate public-review containment implemented in this change. Integration, unified sign-in and site-wide exit notices require separately verified implementation and are not claimed complete here.

## Required experience

Public reviews, insights, help and product explanations belong on company-controlled domains and must not depend on a ChatGPT account or a hosted prototype login. Public reading needs no login. Protected records use the existing approved company identity boundary, role checks and MFA as appropriate; do not disable authentication to remove friction.

Prefer server-side API/webhook integrations with explicit scopes, data minimisation, consent where required and auditable hand-offs. Seamless presentation is not permission to hide processing, forward session tokens, embed private sites or bypass a provider login.

Necessary independent third-party visits require a named destination and a visible, accessible confirmation before the visitor leaves: Stay here / Continue to [provider]. No automatic redirect or preselected consent. Preserve the return journey without exporting tokens or private URL parameters. Distinguish company-controlled ecosystem sites from independent providers; do not describe our own products as unrelated third parties.

Suggested notice, subject to legal review: "You are leaving [site] for [provider] at [domain], an independently operated website. Its terms and privacy policy apply. We do not control that site's content or services. This notice does not limit rights or obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded."

Avoid an absolute 'no responsibility' waiver. ACCC consumer guarantees cannot be removed by a disclaimer: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees . Accessibility reference: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Techniques/general/G201 .
