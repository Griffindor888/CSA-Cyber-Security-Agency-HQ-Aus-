What Solurius brings

Start with the work that matters.

01

Role-relevant learning

Organise learning around the responsibilities, policies and risks people encounter in their actual work.

02

Practice and assessment

Use agreed scenarios and assessments to understand where knowledge translates into action and where support is needed.

03

Targeted improvement

Connect findings to follow-up learning, reassessment and a record of progress that the organisation can review.

Your pathway

From your goal to a practical next step.

Your organisation’s priorities shape the scope, the people involved and the measures of progress.

  1. 01

    Understand your people

    Identify the teams, responsibilities and capability gaps to address.

  2. 02

    Shape the programme

    Agree the learning, practice and assessment appropriate to those roles.

  3. 03

    Learn and apply

    Run the scoped programme with the organisation’s approval and participation.

  4. 04

    Measure and improve

    Review the results, focus support and refine the next learning cycle.

Connected by purpose

How Solurius works within CSA.

Solurius is CSA’s human-capability platform. Where an organisation chooses a connected deployment, learning and assessment evidence can inform WARDALE’s workforce-readiness decisions. Relevant evidence can also support a separately authorised CS Insurance readiness engagement.
