Connected by purpose

How CS Insurance works within CSA.

CS Insurance is CSA’s cyber-risk and insurance-readiness product. With the customer’s permission, relevant evidence from WARDALE and Solurius can support the engagement. Access and information sharing remain defined by that customer’s scope.

Insurance advice, broking, underwriting and placement remain with appropriately licensed or authorised providers. CSiA’s initial pathway supports readiness and enquiry coordination.
